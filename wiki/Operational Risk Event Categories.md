# Operational Risk Event Categories

For risk management and regulatory capital reporting, banks are required to map their historical internal loss data into **seven primary Level 1 event-type categories** defined by the Basel Committee [[raw/OPE.pdf#page=12]].

---

## The 7 Regulatory Event Categories

The following table defines the Level 1 categories, their Level 2 sub-categories, and typical Level 3 activity examples [[raw/OPE.pdf#page=12-14]]:

| Level 1 Event Category | Definition | Level 2 Sub-Categories | Level 3 Activity Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Internal Fraud** | Losses due to acts intended to defraud, misappropriate property, or circumvent regulations/law/policy (excluding diversity/discrimination) involving at least one internal party [[raw/OPE.pdf#page=12]]. | • Unauthorised activity<br>• Theft and fraud | • Mismarking of position (intentional)<br>• Embezzlement / Theft / Robbery<br>• Forgery / Check kiting<br>• Bribes / Kickbacks<br>• Insider trading |
| **2. External Fraud** | Losses due to acts intended to defraud, misappropriate property, or circumvent the law by a third party [[raw/OPE.pdf#page=13]]. | • Theft and fraud<br>• Systems security | • Bank robbery / Theft<br>• Hacking / System breaches<br>• Theft of information (with monetary loss) |
| **3. Employment Practices & Workplace Safety** | Losses arising from acts inconsistent with employment, health, or safety laws/agreements, personal injury claims, or diversity/discrimination [[raw/OPE.pdf#page=13]]. | • Employee relations<br>• Safe environment<br>• Diversity & discrimination | • Compensation, benefit, or termination disputes<br>• Workers' compensation claims<br>• General liability (e.g., slip-and-fall)<br>• All discrimination types |
| **4. Clients, Products & Business Practices** | Losses arising from an unintentional or negligent failure to meet a professional obligation to clients (including suitability/fiduciary), or from product design [[raw/OPE.pdf#page=13]]. | • Suitability, disclosure & fiduciary<br>• Improper market practices<br>• Product flaws<br>• Selection & exposure<br>• Advisory activities | • Fiduciary breaches / Privacy breaches<br>• KYC or disclosure violations<br>• Market manipulation / Insider trading (on firm's account)<br>• Money laundering<br>• Product defects / Model errors |
| **5. Damage to Physical Assets** | Losses arising from loss or damage to physical assets from natural disasters or other external events [[raw/OPE.pdf#page=13]]. | • Disasters and other events | • Natural disaster losses (earthquake, flood)<br>• Terrorism / Vandalism |
| **6. Business Disruption & System Failures** | Losses arising from disruption of business or system failures [[raw/OPE.pdf#page=13]]. | • Systems | • Hardware or software outages<br>• Telecommunications outages<br>• Utility outages / disruptions |
| **7. Execution, Delivery & Process Management** | Losses from failed transaction processing or process management, or from relations with trade counterparties and vendors [[raw/OPE.pdf#page=13]]. | • Transaction capture & execution<br>• Monitoring & reporting<br>• Customer intake/docs<br>• Vendor/Supplier disputes | • Data entry or maintenance errors<br>• Missed deadlines / Delivery failures<br>• Model or system misoperation<br>• Accounting errors<br>• Failed mandatory reporting<br>• Outsourcing / Vendor disputes |

---

## Mapping and Reporting Rules

* **Comprehensive Mapping**: Banks must document their criteria for allocating internal operational losses to these specific event types [[raw/OPE.pdf#page=12]].
* **Boundary Cases (Credit/Market Risks)**:
  * Operational loss events related to credit risk that are accounted for in Credit Risk RWA are **excluded** from the operational loss database. However, credit-related operational losses not accounted for in Credit RWA **must be included** [[raw/OPE.pdf#page=13]].
  * Operational losses related to market risk are **included** and subject to the operational risk standardised approach capital calculation [[raw/OPE.pdf#page=13]].
